### INSTRUMENTATION AND INSIGHTS
- **Strip Counter Issue**: The initial bar was inflated by about 39 due to page-edge shadow runs. The rule for dropping such runs was already in `collation_read.text_runs`, but the counter reimplemented run detection without reading it.
- **S8(a) Tolerance**: The ±0.003 tolerance was below the estimator's resolution. Integer pixel lag over 150px strip centroids gives a quantum of 0.0067. The instrument was fixed with parabolic sub-pixel interpolation, reducing recovery error to ≤0.0004.
- **S6 Failure**: The accuracy improved (0.8760→0.9000 and 0.8375→0.9178), but scored pairs fell from 121 to 90 with 27 orphans. This is a Goodhart pattern, where the score improves by discarding a quarter of the gold. The defect was traced to seeding at 0.25 × profile maximum, which is set by the densest full body line, causing short lines to fall below the threshold.
