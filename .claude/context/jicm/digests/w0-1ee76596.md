## R2.1f ALERT - Catchword Continuity Measurement

**Status:** R2.1f has fired due to the catchword continuity measurement failing to meet the required threshold.

**Instrument:** `collation_read.py` (promoted from `probe_v18.py`)

**Measurement Results:**
- Agreement: 0.312 (5/16)
- Wilson95 lower bound: 0.142
- Required threshold: 0.95

**Key Findings:**
- The head band reader is the primary source of failure, with issues in identifying the first line of text and handling multi-word catchwords.
- Catchwords themselves are reading at 0.87–1.00 accuracy, indicating the issue is not with the OCR but with the measurement approach.

**Next Steps:**
- Redesign the head-side reader to better identify the first baseline or score against the first *n* characters of the next leaf's text block.
- Correct the scoring defect where multi-word catchwords are being miscounted as disagreements.
