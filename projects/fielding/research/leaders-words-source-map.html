Source survey · 27 October 2026

Where the rest of the leaders' words are

You asked what we do not yet have of the words of Latter-day Saint leaders, and where to get it. This page answers that, with the pre-1900 gaps first.

It builds on Fielding's two surveys of 12 October and adds three new research passes from today. Each source is marked by how we can actually get it, not just whether it exists.

1 · The answer

What we do not have, in order of value

  1. 01
    The two worst pre-1900 gaps, 1845–1851 and 1880–1899. No Journal of Discourses, no official Conference Reports. Fill them from: the Frontier Guardian (Orson Hyde, 1849–52), the General Epistles, Orson Pratt's Liverpool pamphlets, the Deseret Weekly's "Discourse" column (verbatim, 1886–98), the official reports of 1897–99, and a transcription project covering all 40 conferences of 1880–1899. free
  2. 02
    Watt's unpublished shorthand sermons, 1851–1874. Hundreds of sermons never printed, including about 235 by Brigham Young. Free to read in the Church History Catalog, but the Church's terms forbid automated download. read only
  3. 03
    Eliza R. Snow's 1,200+ discourses, 1840–1887. The largest body of a Relief Society president's words. Church Historian's Press, free online. read only
  4. 04
    54 complete early periodical runs on archive.org. Elders' Journal, The Prophet, St. Louis Luminary, The Mormon, Western Standard, Nauvoo Neighbor and more, uploaded by the Smith-Pettit Foundation. Clean public-domain text. free
  5. 05
    Pamphlets, tracts and books before 1931. 4,104 Church History Library items dated 1830–99 on archive.org, plus about 40 leaders' books proofread on Project Gutenberg (Talmage, Roberts, Whitney, Widtsoe, Gospel Doctrine). free
  6. 06
    Relief Society Magazine 1914–1970, Instructor 1930–1970, Millennial Star 1901–1970, Young Woman's Journal 1889–1929, Woman's Exponent 1872–1914. Full runs, all free full text. We hold none of these yet. free
  7. 07
    Devotional talks outside conference. BYU (2,545), BYU–Idaho (2,529, from 1957) and BYU–Hawaii (1,437, from 1955). We hold a 27-talk sample of BYU only. free
  8. 08
    Leaders' books published after 1930. Doctrines of Salvation, Gospel Standards, Gospel Ideals, Answers to Gospel Questions, Mormon Doctrine and others. Borrow-only online. buy / borrow
  9. 09
    Conferences the Church site leaves out. Relief Society general conferences and June youth and Primary conferences (to 1975), and the 1970s area conferences. Only in the Ensign, the Relief Society Magazine and the Conference Reports. free read only
  10. 10
    Councils and diaries that record what leaders said. George Q. Cannon's journal (1849–1901), the Nuttall, Roberts, Lund and John Henry Smith diaries, Heber J. Grant's journals. Mostly free; Abraham H. Cannon's diaries must be bought. free buy

Already complete

General Conference from 1971 on. I matched six sample conferences talk for talk against the Church's site. Our copy has every sermon. It leaves out only the sustainings and the audit and statistical reports.

2 · How to read the marks

What each mark means

heldSearchable in our corpus today.
stagedOn our disk, not yet in the corpus.
freeFree full text we may download.
read onlyFree to read, but automated download is barred, or it is page images only.
requestBehind a request, login or reading room.
buy / borrowPurchase, or borrow one copy at a time online.
print onlyNot digitized anywhere we found.

Rights: a work published in or before 1930 is US public domain in 2026. Later works are under copyright unless shown otherwise. That is fine for private research. The text is never republished.

11 · Decisions

Your rulings, and what is still open

  • Restricted typescripts: Include them (your ruling, 27 October). The Minutes of the Apostles, 1835–1893, goes on the acquisition list.
  • Copies not openly available: I search Anna's Archive for every item here that is read only, request-gated, for sale or print only (your ruling). Done in two rounds; see section 12.
  • Held until acquisition is done: The Redis password rotation and the re-reading of the remaining damaged Conference Report pages (your ruling).
  • Still open: Church permission. The Church's terms bar automated download and manual copying without written permission. Ask the Church History Department?
  • Still open: copyright check. Some 1931–1963 books may be public domain through non-renewal. Run a renewal check?
